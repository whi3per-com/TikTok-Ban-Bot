## What the service does

Whisper offers reporting workspaces and hands-on support for legitimate cases that need a clear, structured next step. The service helps organize the situation, the relevant content links, and the outcome being requested so the case can be reviewed with its context.

Available dedicated reporting workflows:

- **[TikTok Reporting Desk](https://whi3per.com/TT-Report/)** — a focused workspace with case setup, target review, and request-status information.
- **[Snapchat Reporting Desk](https://whi3per.com/SC-Report/)** — a separate workflow for legitimate Snapchat reporting cases.
- **[Services & takedown support](https://whi3per.com/Services/)** — direct help for cases that need more context than a self-service workflow. Share the situation, relevant links, and the outcome you are seeking.

## How to get started

1. Open the relevant reporting desk or the [services page](https://whi3per.com/Services/).
2. Describe what happened and provide the direct link to the content or account involved.
3. Explain why you believe the content violates the platform's rules and what outcome you are requesting.
4. Include only relevant supporting context. Redact unrelated personal details and never send passwords, login codes, or account recovery information.
5. Contact the team on [Telegram](https://t.me/WHI3PER) if the case needs human guidance.

## What to expect

Whisper provides a structured reporting path and support for preparing a clear case. **The platform reviews each report and decides whether to take action.** Account restrictions, removals, response times, and outcomes are not guaranteed by Whisper.

Reports should be truthful, specific, and submitted only for legitimate concerns. Do not use reporting workflows for retaliation, harassment, false claims, or coordinated mass reporting.
